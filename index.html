CLIENT AGREEMENT

Client Agreement & Booking Terms

Please review these terms before submitting a booking enquiry. This general template should be legally reviewed before use.

  1. Age: Bookings are available only to adults aged 18 years or older.
  2. Nature of services: BESIDE YOU provides the companionship services described on this website. No illegal activity is offered, arranged, encouraged, or guaranteed.
  3. Mutual consent and boundaries: Every interaction is voluntary. Either party may decline a booking or end an interaction at any time.
  4. Respectful conduct: Harassment, threats, coercion, violence, discrimination, or other unlawful conduct will not be tolerated.
  5. Booking confirmation: Submitting an enquiry does not itself create a confirmed booking. A booking is confirmed only after the details have been mutually agreed.
  6. Cancellation: Either party may cancel or reschedule subject to any cancellation terms communicated before confirmation.
  7. Privacy: Do not submit Aadhaar, PAN, OTPs, passwords, banking credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information through the website.
  8. Legal compliance: All services and interactions must comply with applicable Indian laws. Nothing on this website is intended to authorize or facilitate unlawful activity.
  9. Disputes: Any dispute will be addressed in accordance with applicable law and the terms communicated for the particular booking.

Important: This is a general website template, not legal advice. Obtain a lawyer's review before relying on it as a binding client contract.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is a booking confirmed when I submit the form?

No. A form submission is an enquiry only. Availability and final details must be mutually agreed before a booking is confirmed.

Who can enquire?

BESIDE YOU is for adults aged 18 or older. The website only accepts enquiries for the services described here.

What information should I provide?

Provide your name, WhatsApp number, preferred date, time, duration, area, occasion and a brief message. Do not send Aadhaar, PAN, OTPs, passwords or banking credentials.

Can I request a different service?

Requests are considered only when they fit the services published on this website and applicable law. Requests involving unlawful activity will not be accepted.

How is my enquiry handled?

Enquiries are delivered by a third-party form-processing service to the BESIDE YOU business email. Only information needed to respond to the enquiry should be submitted.

How do I contact BESIDE YOU?

Use the booking form or the WhatsApp contact button on this website. Availability is confirmed individually.
